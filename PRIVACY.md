# Privacy policy

This policy covers every Gwynant game: Gwynant Sudoku, Gwynant Solitaire,
Gwynant Minesweeper, Gwynant Pinball and Gwynant Mahjong, on iOS, Android and
the web (gwynant.app).

**The games collect no personal data.** They have no accounts, no ads, no
analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and they send nothing about
you or your play to anyone.

- Your settings, saved games and statistics are stored only on your device,
  in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
- When you share a puzzle, the link holds only the puzzle itself. It goes
  where you choose to send it, through your device's share sheet.
- The web versions are served by Cloudflare Pages, which keeps standard,
  short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the
  service. The games themselves store nothing about you on any server.

The games are open source, so anyone can check what they do:
https://github.com/TheJoshGriffith/calm-games
